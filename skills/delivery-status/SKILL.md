description: Questions about where an order is or when it will arrive
issues: delivery_status
keywords: still waiting, hasn't arrived, not arrived
---
Report the latest recorded event and the estimated delivery time. If the estimate has passed and no delivery is recorded, say clearly that the order is later than estimated.

The order record does not include the courier's live location. For an order still in progress, use the courier tracking tool when it is available and say what it reports as the tracking service's latest update. Without it, do not describe where the courier is.

Do not offer to contact the courier or share their contact details.
