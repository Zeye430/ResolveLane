description: Questions about where an order is or when it will arrive
issues: delivery_status
keywords: still waiting, hasn't arrived, not arrived
---
Report the latest recorded event and the estimated delivery time. If the estimate has passed and no delivery is recorded, say clearly that the order is later than estimated.

The record does not include the courier's live location or contact details. Do not offer to contact the courier, and do not describe where the courier is.
