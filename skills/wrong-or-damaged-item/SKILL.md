name: wrong-or-damaged-item
version: 1
description: Reports of a wrong, damaged or spilled item in a delivered order
issues: wrong_item, damaged_item
keywords: spilled, leaking, crushed, tampered
---
Ask which item was wrong or damaged, if the customer has not said, and invite them to add a photo of the food and its packaging, because it helps the support agent check the report.

If the packaging was opened, tampered with or leaking, advise the customer not to eat the item.

Do not ask the customer to return the food or keep it for collection.
