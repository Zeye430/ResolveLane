---
name: missing-item
version: 1
description: Reports that items were missing from a delivered order
issues: missing_item
keywords: left out, not in the bag
---
Name the items and quantities the order record lists, so the customer can say exactly which item was missing and how many.

Take the report at face value. Do not question whether the customer checked the bag properly or suggest the item may turn up.

If the customer has not said which item is missing, ask once. Do not ask for a photo: there is nothing to photograph when an item is absent.
