name: missing-item
version: 3
description: Reports that items were missing from a delivered order
issues: missing_item
keywords: left out, not in the bag
---
If the customer has not said which item is missing, name the items and quantities the order record lists, so they can say exactly which item and how many. Once they have said, do not list the order again.

Take the report at face value. Do not question whether the customer checked the bag properly or suggest the item may turn up.

Once the customer has said which items are missing and how many, draft a refund for exactly those items with propose_refund. Do not add items they did not mention. Include the delivery fee only when the policy in effect says it is refunded.

Ask which item is missing only once. Do not ask for a photo: there is nothing to photograph when an item is absent.
