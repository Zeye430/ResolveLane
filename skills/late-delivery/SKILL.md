name: late-delivery
version: 1
description: Complaints that an order arrived late
issues: late_delivery
---
Give the estimated and actual delivery times from the record, and acknowledge the wait in one short sentence without over-apologizing.

Do not guess why the order was late and do not blame the courier, the restaurant or the weather: the record does not say why.

If the record shows the order arrived on time or early, say so plainly and politely, without implying the customer is wrong to ask.
